🌹 Rose

Queen of Flowers

About the Rose

The rose has symbolized love, beauty, and courage for thousands of years. There are over 300 known species of roses found throughout the world. Red roses symbolize romance. White roses symbolize purity. Yellow roses symbolize friendship. Pink roses symbolize admiration.

🌷 Tulip

Spring's Favorite Flower

About Tulips

Tulips originated in Central Asia and later became famous in the Netherlands. They bloom every spring in many brilliant colors and symbolize perfect love, renewal, and happiness.

🌻 Sunflower

Nature's Sunshine

About Sunflowers

Young sunflowers actually follow the sun across the sky. This movement is called heliotropism. Sunflowers represent loyalty, positivity, and happiness.

🪷 Lotus

Flower of Purity

About Lotus

The lotus grows from muddy water yet blooms perfectly clean. It represents peace, purity, wisdom, and spiritual awakening in many cultures.

🌼 Daisy

Flower of Joy

About Daisies

Daisies symbolize innocence, hope, and fresh beginnings. Their bright petals attract bees and butterflies that help pollinate gardens.

🌺 Hibiscus

Tropical Beauty

About Hibiscus

Hibiscus flowers are famous for their large colorful blooms. They are also used to make refreshing herbal tea around the world.
